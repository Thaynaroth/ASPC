# ASPC - Automation Selling Platform in Cambodia
ASPC is the next generation of "Automation Selling Platform in Cambodia", focusing on Affordability powered by AI integration and Telegram Bot (the most-used messaging platform in Cambodia). Our mission is to automate selling — from giving customers information about the shop, products, and buying process, through to ordering products, creating invoices, payment with KHQR, selecting delivery services, generating reports, and receiving customer complaints.

By bringing the whole team of Sales, Marketing, and Consulting together in one platform.
